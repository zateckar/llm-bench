Performance

One 1,024-token reference workload, 256-token output limit, warm connections, one attempt per timed request. Concurrency increases automatically. Throughput counts successful output over elapsed time including failures. Latency and TTFT percentiles cover successful requests. TTFT measures first delivered content or reasoning; provider buffering can affect it. This is a bounded concurrency test.
