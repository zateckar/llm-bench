Capacity method and limits {{ capacity.revision }} · provisional

We select the fastest tested point meeting p95 first-token/response latency, the streaming rate target and at least 99% complete answers. Counts must be reported and streaming evidence must cover every complete answer. Context and output length must match within 25%; longer-context workloads require their own measurements. Cold and warm prefixes and reasoning settings remain separate. Four samples are the minimum; small samples and short tests leave tail latency and sustained capacity uncertain.

Planned request rate = min(measured requests/s, measured output tokens/s ÷ target output, measured input tokens/s ÷ modeled input per request) × (1 − headroom). Mean in-flight requests = planned requests/s × measured mean response seconds. Peak active users = planned output tokens/min ÷ average token demand per user. Tool calls count as model requests; multiple simultaneous calls from one agent consume multiple slots.

Daily requests = planned requests/s × availability × min(busy seconds ÷ busy traffic share ÷ peak factor, quiet seconds ÷ quiet traffic share). The 24-hour flat column assumes the planned rate all day. Both are volume estimates, not measured daily production. Busy/quiet traffic patterns are assumptions until a representative multi-day history is available.
